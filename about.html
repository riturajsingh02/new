01 WHO WE ARE
THE MAKHANAM STORY

Connecting the authentic origin of Foxnut with modern global enterprise.

Makhanam is a premier processing and wholesale supply establishment founded in the wetland heartlands of Bihar, India.

We bridge traditional harvesting wisdom with modern sorting, grading, and moisture-controlled packaging. Our mission is to deliver uniform, handpicked raw Makhana to commercial brands, distributors, and exporters across India and international markets.

From initial pond harvesting to machine calibration and laboratory quality checks, every shipment adheres to stringent B2B specifications.

GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION Mithila, Bihar (India)
OUR HERITAGE

From the pristine wetlands to your supply chain.

Over 85% of the world’s Makhana originates in northern Bihar. Makhanam operates at the direct source in Bihar, eliminating redundant intermediary layers to guarantee peak freshness, ethical farmer partnerships, and cost advantages for bulk buyers.

OUR METHODOLOGY

From raw harvest to certified shipment.

A rigorous 4-step quality lifecycle ensuring uncompromised bulk consistency.

01

Wetland Harvesting & Sun Drying

Hand-harvested gorgon nut seeds cleaned and systematically sun-cured in the Mithila wetlands.

02

Artisanal Roasting & Popping

Precise thermal expansion ensuring optimal white puff expansion with structural integrity.

03

Traditional Suta & Caliper Grading

Multi-deck sieving categorizing inventory precisely into 3 Suta Pearl (9–12.7mm), 4 Suta Classic (12.7–15.8mm), 5 Suta Royal (15.9–19.0mm), 6+ Suta Jumbo (19–26mm), and 7 Suta Super Jumbo (>21mm).

04

Vacuum Barrier Bulk Packaging

Sealed in heavy-duty HDPE / master cartons for domestic freight and international container export.
