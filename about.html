SEED TO SHIPMENT

The 4-Stage Quality Lifecycle

Visualizing how every single Foxnut is cultivated, harvested, expanded, and calibrated under strict standards.

Stage 01

Wetland Cultivation

Naturally grown in mineral-rich standing waters of northern Bihar without synthetic growth agents.

Stage 02

Underwater Harvest

Hand-gathered from muddy pond beds by skilled generational Mallah divers, then thoroughly water-cleaned.

Stage 03

Artisanal Popping

High-heat roasting and precise manual striking expand the dark shell into delicate, snow-white Makhana.

Stage 04

Caliper Grading

Multi-deck mechanical sieving categorizes lots from 3 Suta Pearl to 7 Suta Super Jumbo before sealed pack.
