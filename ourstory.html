The Story of Makhanam

Over 85% of the world’s Foxnut originates in the freshwater wetlands of northern Bihar. Makhanam was established to unite ancestral aquatic harvesting wisdom with scientific Suta calibration — delivering pure single-origin raw Makhana directly to enterprise buyers worldwide.

Founder

Keshav Kumar Yadav

Rooted in Mithila’s harvesting terroir, Keshav founded Makhanam to establish direct farmgate partnerships with Mallah harvesting families — eliminating intermediaries, ensuring fair trade, and safeguarding the authentic wetland provenance of true Foxnuts.

Single-Origin Terroir · Direct Farmgate Transparency · Wetland Heritage Stewardship
Chief Executive Officer

Anupam Kumari

Championing industrial precision and institutional compliance, Anupam steers Makhanam’s enterprise supply chain — standardizing scientific Suta millimeter calibration, certified moisture control (< 7.5%), and seamless fulfillment for global B2B food manufacturers.

Enterprise B2B Supply Chain · Precision Suta Sorting · FSSAI & Export Compliance
